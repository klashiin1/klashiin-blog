# Elden Ring DEX Build Guide

## Overview
A Dexterity-focused build emphasizing speed, poise-breaking, and critical damage.

## Build Overview

## Stats Priority
1. **Dexterity** (80+) - Primary damage scaling
2. **Vigor** (50-60) - Health survivability
3. **Endurance** (30-40) - Stamina for attacking/rolling
4. **Mind** (20-30) - FP for spells/abilities
5. **Strength** (12-18) - Minimum for weapon requirements

## Recommended Weapons
- Katanas (Uchigatana, Nagakiba)
- Curved Swords (Scavenger's Curved Sword)
- Rapiers (Rapier, Nagakiba)
- Twin Blades (Godskin Peeler)

## Talismans
- Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman
- Ritual Pot Talisman
- Millicent's Prosthesis
- Claw Mark Seal

## Spells & Ash of War
- **Corpse Piler** or **Bloodhound's Step**
- Sepulcher Finger
- Carrian Retaliation

## Tips
- Use jumping heavy attacks for crowd control
- Wear light armor for poise management
- Dual-wield for maximum DPS
- Upgrade weapons to +25 (regular) or +10 (special)
